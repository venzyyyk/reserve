## Context

The forces in play: the constraint, the deadline, the measurement, the
product requirement. Written so a reader in a year understands the situation
without asking anyone. State facts and numbers, not conclusions.

## Decision

What we are doing, in the active voice. Specific enough that a reviewer can
tell whether a given pull request complies.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
| ------ | ------- |
| …      | …       |

Every ADR must list at least one rejected alternative, including — where it
applies — "do nothing", with the reason it loses.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what we accept as the cost.
Include the trigger that should make us revisit the decision.
